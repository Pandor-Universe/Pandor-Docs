PANDOR is free to play. Premium adds real mining packs, the right to burn dust for the weekly prize, and two planning tools: the auto queue and the campaign planner.

## Price

| | |
|---|---|
| Price | 20 USDC |
| Length | 30 days |
| Paid with | One USDC payment from your wallet |
| Renewing | Adds 30 days on top of what you have left |

Premium belongs to your planet. Buy it in the app with **Go premium**. To renew, tap **Add thirty days**. The new days start where your current premium ends, so renewing early never loses you a day.

You can also buy premium and a small mining pack together, with one approval.

The whole 20 USDC goes to the PANDOR treasury.

## What premium unlocks

| Unlock | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mining packs** | Buy small and big packs that earn pORE, which you can take out as ORE. Your planet needs live premium at the moment you buy. See [Mining](/ore/mining). |
| **Burn dust** | Burn dust to climb the weekly board and share the weekly prize. See [Weekly prize](/ore/weekly-prize). |
| **Auto queue** | Line up build and training orders. RHEA works through them in order, even while the app is closed. See [Orders](/planet/orders). |
| **Campaign planner** | Plan a strike where up to 13 fleets land on one target at the same moment. See [Coalitions](/war/coalitions). |

## What stays free

Everything else is free, with or without premium:

- claiming your planet and playing the game;
- automatic defence, which RHEA runs for you (see [Raids](/war/raids));
- the [simulation pack](/ore/mining#the-free-simulation-pack), which keeps your planet making dust;
- collecting dust;
- rolling [Supply Drops](/ore/supply-drops), which cost SKR but need no premium;
- taking out ORE, and claiming a weekly prize you've already won.

## When premium ends

The app shows **Premium ended**. From then on:

- Packs you already bought keep mining to the end of their seven days.
- The auto queue pauses. Your orders stay in it and pick up again when you renew.
- Your fleets that haven't left yet drop out of planned strikes.
- You can't buy new packs or burn dust until you renew.

## Other ways to get premium days

Each of these adds days on top of whatever premium you have left, the same way a purchase does.

| Source | Premium days | How often |
|---|---|---|
| [Seeker Genesis](/seeker-genesis) trial | 7 | Once per token, ever |
| A [Supply Drop](/ore/supply-drops) premium prize | 1 to 3 | A 2.5% chance on each drop |
