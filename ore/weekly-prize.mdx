Each week, premium planets burn dust to climb a board. The top ten share that week's prize. It's paid in **pORE**, which you can take out as ORE.

## The week

A week runs from **Thursday 00:00 UTC** to the next Thursday 00:00 UTC. Each week has its own board and its own prize.

## Burn dust

Burning dust needs live [premium](/ore/premium) on your planet.

- Tap **Burn dust** and choose any whole amount.
- It comes from your tank first, then your balance. See [Dust](/planet/dust).
- RHEA signs the burn with your planet's game key, so there's no wallet prompt. Before burning, RHEA credits everything your packs have earned so far, so all your dust is there to burn.
- Burned dust is gone for good. It counts only for the week you burn it in.

Your score is all the dust you burned that week. The board shows the **Top 10**. If two planets tie, the one that reached the score first ranks higher.

## Your share

Each planet in the top ten gets a share of the prize in line with how much it burned:

> your share = the prize × your dust burned ÷ all the dust burned by the top ten, rounded down

So if you burned a quarter of the top ten's dust, your share is a quarter of the prize. Then the cap applies.

While the week runs, **Your share of this week's prize** shows what you'd get if the week ended now. It moves as others burn.

## The cap

You can never win more than the pORE your planet's **paid packs** mined that week. The app calls this **The most you can win**.

- Only real [mining packs](/ore/mining) count. The free simulation pack never does.
- So with only the free pack, you can top the board and still win nothing.
- pORE counts in the week it's recorded. RHEA records it about once an hour, and once more just before the week ends.

What a capped winner can't take is not shared out among the others. It rolls into the prize of the week in progress when the board is counted. The app shows it as **Carried into the next week**.

<Tip>
To win anything, you need all three in the same week: premium, a place in the top ten, and pORE mined by a paid pack.
</Tip>

## Claim your prize

Shortly after the week ends, the board is counted. Until then, the app shows that week's shares as being counted.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the week">
    Your result appears under **Past weeks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim with your payout wallet">
    Tap **Claim**, which shows your prize in pORE. Only your confirmed [payout wallet](/wallet/what-you-sign#your-payout-wallet) can claim. If you haven't confirmed it yet, you can confirm and claim with one signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it out as ORE">
    The pORE lands in your wallet. Take it out as ORE under **Mine**, like any pORE. See [Take out ORE](/ore/mining#take-out-ore).
  </Step>
</Steps>

You have **four weeks** after a week ends to claim its prize, and each prize can be claimed once. After four weeks, an unclaimed prize rolls into the prize of the week in progress.
