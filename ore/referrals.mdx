**Coming soon.** Referrals arrive in the next app update. They aren't live yet.

Invite a friend to PANDOR, and you both get the same reward.

## The reward

| | Your friend | You |
|---|---:|---:|
| [Supply Drops](/ore/supply-drops) | 3 | 3 |
| Days of [premium](/ore/premium) | 3 | 3 |

The drops are in-game Supply Drops, the same drops you can roll in the app.

## How it will work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your friend joins PANDOR">
    Give them your invite code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They enter your code in the app">
    They have 7 days from joining to enter your invite code. Your friend gets their reward as soon as they enter it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get yours">
    You're rewarded when your friend holds a Seeker Genesis Token, or makes a first purchase: premium, a mining pack or a paid Supply Drop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can be rewarded for up to **10 invites**.
