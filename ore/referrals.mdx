Invite a friend to PANDOR, and you both get the same reward.

## The reward

| | Your friend | You |
|---|---:|---:|
| [Supply Drops](/ore/supply-drops) | 3 | 3 |
| Days of [premium](/ore/premium) | 3 | 3 |

The drops are in-game Supply Drops, the same drops you can roll in the app.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your friend joins PANDOR">
    Your code is on the **Invite a friend** card on your Planet deck. Tap it to copy, or tap **Share invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They enter your code in the app">
    They can enter it right after claiming their planet, or later on the same card, within 7 days of claiming. One code, once, and never their own. Your friend gets their reward as soon as they enter it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get yours">
    You're rewarded when your friend holds a Seeker Genesis Token, or makes a first purchase: premium, a mining pack or a paid Supply Drop. You get an **A friend joined** alert.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can be rewarded for up to **10 invites**.
