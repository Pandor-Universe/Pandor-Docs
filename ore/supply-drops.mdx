**Supply Drops, the in-game gacha,** each hold one prize for your planet: dust, troops, a fast-track token or premium days. Drops hold in-game items only. They never contain tokens.

## Price

| | |
|---|---|
| Price | 20 SKR a drop |
| Buy | **Roll ×1** or **Roll ×10** |
| Paid by | Your planet's payout wallet, in SKR |
| Needs premium | No |

A roll of ten drops is still a single payment, and your SKR goes to the PANDOR treasury. Your planet can have one roll opening at a time. Roll again once it has opened.

The price is set on chain, and a roll never charges more than the price the app shows. If the price ever changes, the app asks you to update before you roll again.

If your payout wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token, you get free drops, used before any SKR. See [Seeker Genesis](/seeker-genesis).

## What's inside

| Prize | Chance | You get | Card |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Dust | 60% | 500 dust | Common |
| Dust | 17% | 5,000 dust | Uncommon |
| Dust | 3% | 50,000 dust | Rare |
| Troops | 15% | 3 to 10 troops of one type | Troops |
| Fast-track token | 2.5% | 1 token | Rare |
| Premium | 2.5% | 1 to 3 days | Rare · premium |

The app shows these odds under **Odds on-chain**: they're stored on chain.

### Dust

Dust goes straight into your balance, not your tank. See [Dust](/planet/dust).

### Troops

You get 3 to 10 troops of one type: raider, heavy or defender, named for your planet's species. They join your garrison at once and pay upkeep from then on, like trained troops.

| Species | Raider | Heavy | Defender |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terra | Falcon | Titan | Aegis |
| Ferox | Skirmisher | Dreadnought | Rampart |
| Vex | Wraith | Marauder | Ward |
| Bastion | Harrier | Juggernaut | Bulwark |

Upkeep is 10 dust an hour for a raider, 30 for a heavy and 20 for a defender. If your planet is insolvent, you get the troops' cost in dust instead: 200 dust per raider, 350 per heavy and 300 per defender. The card then says **Troops as dust**. See [Fleet](/war/fleet).

### Fast-track token

A fast-track token finishes your running upgrade at once. It's kept on chain until you use it. While an upgrade is building, the app offers **Finish now · 1 fast-track**, and RHEA spends the token with your planet's game key, with no wallet prompt. The offer goes away 15 seconds before the upgrade is due, so a token is never wasted on a finished build. See [Upgrades](/planet/upgrades).

### Premium days

One to three days, added on top of any premium you have. See [Premium](/ore/premium).

## How a drop is decided

ORE's own on-chain round result decides what's inside. Not you, and not PANDOR.

1. You pay first.
2. Your roll is tied to an ORE mining round that hasn't ended yet. When you pay, nobody knows that round's result.
3. When the round ends, ORE's round result opens every drop in your roll. It's the same result that decides ORE's own payouts.

Each drop's prize is worked out from that round result, your planet and the drop's number, so anyone can check it.

## How long a drop takes

A drop opens when its ORE round ends. Expect anything from under a minute to about a minute and a half. You can leave the screen: the result waits for you.

Your wallet signs each roll. PANDOR usually covers the network fee, and the app tells you when it does. See [What you sign](/wallet/what-you-sign).
