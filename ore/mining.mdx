Packs are how your planet earns over time. The free simulation pack pays dust. Real mining packs pay dust too, and earn pORE that you can take out as ORE.

| | Simulation pack | Small pack | Big pack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | 20 USDC | 200 USDC |
| Needs premium | No | Yes | Yes |
| Runs for | 7 days | 7 days | 7 days |
| Pays | Dust only | Dust and pORE | Dust and pORE, from 10 times the SOL |

<Note>
With no pack running, your planet makes no dust over time. Keep the free simulation pack going.
</Note>

## The free simulation pack

Under **Mine**, tap **Start simulation pack**. There's nothing to pay or sign: RHEA runs it with your planet's game key.

It runs for seven days and pays **dust only, never ORE or pORE**, at your planet's normal production rate:

> dust per minute = 50 × production level × species bonus × rarity bonus, rounded down

The species bonus is 1.25 for Terra and 1 for the others. The rarity bonus is 1 for Common, 1.05 for Rare and 1.10 for Legendary.

| Dust per minute | Level 1 | Level 5 | Level 10 | Level 20 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Common | 50 | 250 | 500 | 1,000 |
| Rare | 52 | 262 | 525 | 1,050 |
| Legendary | 55 | 275 | 550 | 1,100 |
| Terra, Common | 62 | 312 | 625 | 1,250 |
| Terra, Rare | 65 | 328 | 656 | 1,312 |
| Terra, Legendary | 68 | 343 | 687 | 1,375 |

The first three rows are for Ferox, Vex and Bastion. An hour is 60 times as much: 3,000 dust for a Common planet at level 1. To raise your level, see [Upgrades](/planet/upgrades).

You run one simulation pack at a time. When it ends, tap **Restart simulation pack** for another seven days. It never restarts on its own.

The screen also shows **It plays as**, a small pack's price, and **ORE it would have mined**: a preview of what a real small pack would have mined. The simulation pack never pays it out.

## Real mining packs

Real packs mine through **RubyPool's Perpetua**, an ORE mining pool on Solana. RubyPool mines the ORE, and your position earns **pORE**, RubyPool's token, which you can take out as ORE.

To buy a pack, your planet needs live [premium](/ore/premium), and your [payout wallet](/wallet/what-you-sign#your-payout-wallet) pays in USDC, with one approval. You can buy premium and a small pack together.

| Where the price goes | Share | Small pack | Big pack |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Your own RubyPool deposit, which mines for you | 80% | 16 USDC | 160 USDC |
| PANDOR treasury | 10% | 2 USDC | 20 USDC |
| PANDOR's bonus wallet | 10% | 2 USDC | 20 USDC |

Nothing is swapped when you buy. Your 80% stays USDC, in a RubyPool deposit in your wallet's name.

<Note>
Your first pack also opens your RubyPool accounts, which take a deposit of about 0.007 SOL. If your wallet doesn't have it, PANDOR sends that SOL for you, once per wallet. Most of it comes back to your wallet when your deposit closes.
</Note>

### Your USDC goes in over seven days

RubyPool takes your USDC in equal steps over seven days. It turns each step into SOL and puts it into your own position, so your pack mines all week. The app shows **Your USDC, going in**, with the size of each step and how much is still to go in.

- A small pack goes in as 32 steps of 0.5 USDC, one every 5 h 15 min.
- A big pack goes in as 320 steps of 0.5 USDC, one every 31 min 30 s.

If you buy another pack while one is still going in, the USDC still waiting comes back to your wallet in the same purchase and joins the new pack's. It all goes in over a fresh seven days.

Once it's in, it's used up by mining. You don't get the USDC back: you get pORE.

### What a real pack earns

- **pORE, in your wallet's name.** Your position belongs to your payout wallet, and only that wallet can take out what it earns.
- **Dust from pORE.** 1,900,000 dust for each ORE's worth of pORE your packs mine, plus 10% for each production level above 1.
- **The base rate.** While a real pack runs, your planet makes the same dust per minute as the simulation pack. You never get two base rates at once.

## Take out ORE

Under **Mine**, tap **Take out ORE**, choose an amount or **Everything**, and approve once in your wallet.

- You get **88.75% of your pORE's ORE value**. By RubyPool's fee schedule, the rest is ORE's 10% refining fee plus RubyPool's 1.25%.
- You can take out any time, up to what RubyPool can pay out right now. If you ask for more, the app offers the most it can.
- A take-out can wait briefly, shown as submitted, while RubyPool syncs with an ORE round. It's sent as soon as RubyPool is ready. If it waits too long, see the [FAQ](/wallet/faq).

pORE you win in the [weekly prize](/ore/weekly-prize) lands in your wallet, and you take it out the same way.
