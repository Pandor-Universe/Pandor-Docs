Packs are how your planet earns over time. The free simulation pack pays dust. Real mining packs pay dust too, and mine ORE that you can take out.

| | Simulation pack | Small pack | Big pack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | 20 USDC | 200 USDC |
| Needs premium | No | Yes | Yes |
| Runs for | 7 days | 7 days | 7 days |
| Pays | Dust only | Dust and ORE | Dust and ORE, from 10 times the SOL |

<Note>
With no pack running, your planet makes no dust over time. Keep the free simulation pack going.
</Note>

## The free simulation pack

In the **Mining box** on your Planet deck, or under **Mine**, tap **Start simulation pack**. There's nothing to pay or sign: RHEA runs it with your planet's game key.

It runs for seven days and pays **dust only, never ORE**, at your planet's normal production rate:

> dust per minute = 25 × production level × species bonus × rarity bonus, rounded down

The species bonus is 1.25 for Terra and 1 for the others. The rarity bonus is 1 for Common, 1.05 for Rare and 1.10 for Legendary.

| Dust per minute | Level 1 | Level 5 | Level 10 | Level 20 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Common | 25 | 125 | 250 | 500 |
| Rare | 26 | 131 | 262 | 525 |
| Legendary | 27 | 137 | 275 | 550 |
| Terra, Common | 31 | 156 | 312 | 625 |
| Terra, Rare | 32 | 164 | 328 | 656 |
| Terra, Legendary | 34 | 171 | 343 | 687 |

The first three rows are for Ferox, Vex and Bastion. An hour is 60 times as much: 1,500 dust for a Common planet at level 1. To raise your level, see [Upgrades](/planet/upgrades).

You run one simulation pack at a time. It renews itself every seven days, so you only start it once.

The screen also shows **It plays as**, a small pack's price, and **ORE it would have mined**: a preview of what a real small pack would have mined. The simulation pack never pays it out.

## Real mining packs

Real packs mine through **RubyPool's Perpetua**, an ORE mining pool on Solana. RubyPool mines the ORE, and your position builds up your share of it, which you can take out.

To buy a pack, your planet needs live [premium](/ore/premium), and your [payout wallet](/wallet/what-you-sign#your-payout-wallet) pays in USDC, with one approval. You can buy premium and a small pack together.

| Where the price goes | Share | Small pack | Big pack |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Your own RubyPool deposit, which mines for you | 80% | 16 USDC | 160 USDC |
| PANDOR treasury | 10% | 2 USDC | 20 USDC |
| PANDOR's bonus wallet | 10% | 2 USDC | 20 USDC |

Your 80% goes into a RubyPool deposit in your wallet's name, as USDC.

### Short of USDC?

If your wallet holds SOL, the same order can swap just enough of it for the USDC you're missing. The USDC you hold goes first. Before your wallet opens, the app says how much USDC and SOL the order will use. If your wallet can't cover it, **Buy** stays greyed out.

On an older Seed Vault, an order that uses SOL can ask for two approvals instead of one.

<Note>
Your first pack also opens your RubyPool accounts, which take a deposit of about 0.007 SOL. If your wallet doesn't have it, PANDOR sends that SOL for you, once per wallet. Most of it comes back to your wallet when your deposit closes.
</Note>

### Your USDC goes in over seven days

RubyPool takes your USDC in equal steps over seven days. It turns each step into SOL and puts it into your own position, so your pack mines all week. The app shows **Your USDC, going in**, with the size of each step and how much is still to go in.

- A small pack goes in as 32 steps of 0.5 USDC, one every 5 h 15 min.
- A big pack goes in as 320 steps of 0.5 USDC, one every 31 min 30 s.

If you buy another pack while one is still going in, the USDC still waiting comes back to your wallet in the same purchase and joins the new pack's. It all goes in over a fresh seven days.

Once it's in, it's used up by mining. You don't get the USDC back: you get the ORE it mines.

### What a real pack earns

- **ORE, in your wallet's name.** Your position belongs to your payout wallet, and only that wallet can take out what it mines.
- **Dust from mining.** 13,300,000 dust for each ORE your packs mine, plus 20% for each production level above 1. This is most of the dust a real pack earns.
- **The base rate.** While a real pack runs, your planet makes the same dust per minute as the simulation pack. You never get two base rates at once.

### In numbers

Dust per hour for a Common planet, as a projection:

| Level | Simulation pack (free) | One small pack | One big pack |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1,500 | about 9,500 | about 81,000 |
| 5 | 7,500 | about 22,000 | about 151,000 |
| 10 | 15,000 | about 37,000 | about 238,000 |
| 20 | 30,000 | about 68,000 | about 413,000 |

The free column is exact. The pack columns are estimates: they assume a small pack mines about 0.0006 ORE an hour and a big pack ten times that, close to RubyPool's recent rate. What a pack really mines changes with ORE's rounds, so your numbers will move around these. Terra, Rare and Legendary planets earn more on the base rate (see the table above); the dust from mining is the same for every species and rarity.

## Take out ORE

Under **Mine**, tap **Redeem ORE**. It takes out everything. RHEA tells you about how much ORE you'll receive, and you approve once in your wallet.

- You get **88.75% of what your position is worth in ORE**. By RubyPool's fee schedule, the rest is ORE's 10% refining fee plus RubyPool's 1.25%.
- You can redeem any time, up to what RubyPool can pay out in one go. If everything is more than that, the app offers the most it can.
- A take-out can wait briefly, shown as submitted, while RubyPool syncs with an ORE round. It's sent as soon as RubyPool is ready. If it waits too long, see the [FAQ](/wallet/faq).

Prizes from the [weekly prize](/ore/weekly-prize) arrive in your wallet as ORE, so there's nothing to take out.
