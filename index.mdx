PANDOR is a real-time space strategy game on Solana, made for the Seeker phone. You run one planet. You build it up, train a fleet, raid rival worlds for dust, team up in coalitions, and mine real ORE.

RHEA is the game's AI. You give the orders, and she carries them out, including while the app is closed. She also defends your planet for you. She holds your planet's game key, so building, training and raiding never ask your wallet to sign. Your wallet signs only a handful of money matters, like premium, mining packs and prize claims: see [what you sign](/wallet/what-you-sign). In the app, you're the Commander. Tap her chat head to ask her about the game: see [Ask RHEA](/planet/orders#ask-rhea).

PANDOR is free to play. Your planet is free, and the free simulation pack keeps it making dust. Premium adds real mining packs, which earn real ORE, and tools that carry out your plans ahead of time.

## The loop

1. **Mine.** Keep a pack running. It's the only way your planet makes dust over time.
2. **Build.** Upgrade the Dust Reactor to make more dust, and the Bastion, your defence building, to hold off raids.
3. **Train.** Raise raiders, heavies and defenders. Every troop eats dust every hour.
4. **Raid.** Hit rival worlds and bring their dust home.

Then do it again, bigger. Join a coalition to reinforce friends and land strikes together. With premium, burn dust for a share of the weekly prize.

<Warning>
There's no beginner shield. From your first minute, any planet can attack yours.
</Warning>

## Where the game runs

PANDOR runs where each part works best: fast where you fight, on Solana where you own.

| On MagicBlock's Ephemeral Rollup | On Solana mainnet |
|---|---|
| Training | Your planet NFT |
| Upgrades | Every payment |
| Raids and defence | ORE mining |
| Spending dust | The weekly ORE prize |

The rollup is public, with your troop counts and spendable dust encrypted, and it syncs back to Solana mainnet. Moves there are free.
