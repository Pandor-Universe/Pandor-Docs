Dust is PANDOR's in-game currency. You spend it on upgrades and troops. Your troops eat it as upkeep. Raiders come to take it. Every planet starts with 25,000.

## Dust is made while a pack runs

Your planet makes dust over time only while a pack is running.

- **The free simulation pack.** Under **Mine**, tap **Start simulation pack**. It's free and your wallet signs nothing. It runs for 7 days and then renews itself, so you only start it once.
- **A real mining pack.** Paid packs pay the same dust rate while they run, on top of what they mine. You never earn two rates at once. See [Mining](/ore/mining).

While a pack runs, your planet makes:

> 25 × production level × species bonus × rarity bonus, in dust per minute, rounded down

Terra's species bonus is ×1.25. Every other species is ×1. Rarity is ×1 for Common, ×1.05 for Rare and ×1.10 for Legendary.

Dust per hour, while a pack runs:

| Level | Common | Rare | Legendary | Terra Common | Terra Rare | Terra Legendary |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1,500 | 1,560 | 1,620 | 1,860 | 1,920 | 2,040 |
| 2 | 3,000 | 3,120 | 3,300 | 3,720 | 3,900 | 4,080 |
| 5 | 7,500 | 7,860 | 8,220 | 9,360 | 9,840 | 10,260 |
| 10 | 15,000 | 15,720 | 16,500 | 18,720 | 19,680 | 20,580 |
| 20 | 30,000 | 31,500 | 33,000 | 37,500 | 39,360 | 41,220 |

The first three columns are Ferox, Vex and Bastion worlds. A real mining pack adds the dust from the ORE it mines on top: see [Mining, in numbers](/ore/mining#in-numbers). To raise your level, see [Upgrades](/planet/upgrades).

<Warning>
With no pack running, your planet makes no dust, but upkeep keeps running. A planet with troops and no pack slides into debt.
</Warning>

## In the tank and your balance

Your dust sits in two places.

| | In the tank | Your balance |
|---|---|---|
| What lands there | Dust from your packs | Collected dust, loot, Supply Drop dust |
| Who can see it | Anyone: it's public on chain | Other players can't: it's encrypted |
| When you're raided | Moved into your balance before the raid lands | Where a winning raid takes its loot |
| When you spend | Collected first, automatically | Spent from here |

Pack dust reaches your tank about once an hour, and right away before you spend or burn dust. The tank holds about 3 billion dust, so in practice it never fills.

The **Dust** figure at the top of the app is everything together: your balance, your tank, and pack dust that hasn't reached the tank yet. Beside it, **Production** shows your dust per hour, with your net after upkeep underneath. **Upkeep** shows what your troops eat per hour.

## Collecting

Collecting moves dust from your tank into your balance. It's free, and your wallet never signs. It happens in two ways:

- **Collect.** Open Settings, then the Dust screen ("Your tank, and collecting it."), and tap **Collect**.
- **Automatically, when you act.** Every order that spends dust (training, upgrades, clearing a debt) and every attack you send collects your tank first.

There's no timer. Dust stays in the tank until you spend, collect, or a raid lands on you.

Collecting doesn't protect dust from raids, because your tank moves into your balance before a raid lands anyway. What it does change is who can see it: anyone can read what's in your tank, but your balance is encrypted.

## What dust pays for

| Spent on | How much |
|---|---|
| Upgrades | 1,000 × level² for each level. See [Upgrades](/planet/upgrades). |
| Troops | 200 a raider, 350 a heavy, 300 a defender. See [Fleet](/war/fleet). |
| Upkeep | 1, 3 or 2 per troop per hour, taken continuously |
| Burning | Premium only. **Burn dust** for the [weekly prize](/ore/weekly-prize): the tank first, then your balance. Burned dust is gone for good. |
| Loot | What raiders carry off when they beat your defence |

Premium, mining packs and Supply Drops are paid from your wallet in USDC, SOL or SKR, never in dust.

## Where dust comes from

- Your 25,000 starting dust.
- Your pack, while it runs.
- Real mining: the ORE your paid packs mine also brings dust. See [Mining](/ore/mining).
- Loot from attacks you win, added when your survivors land.
- [Supply Drops](/ore/supply-drops): prizes of 500, 5,000 or 50,000 dust, paid straight into your balance.

## Upkeep

Every troop eats dust every hour: 1 for a raider, 3 for a heavy, 2 for a defender. It comes out of your balance a little at a time, all day.

- It starts the moment you pay for troops, not when they finish training.
- It keeps running while troops are away attacking.
- It stops when troops die in battle or starve.

Ten defenders cost 20 dust an hour. Check the **Upkeep** figure before you train a big batch. More on [Fleet](/war/fleet).

## Debt

Upkeep can push your balance below zero. A raid can't: it never takes more than your balance holds (see [Loot](/war/raids#loot)). Once your balance and tank together are below zero, your planet can be flagged as in debt, shown as **Owed**. While it's flagged:

- you can't upgrade, train, attack, reinforce, or burn dust from your balance;
- your planet defends at **half strength**;
- Supply Drop troops arrive as their price in dust instead.

Debt doesn't last. If your production, loot or Supply Drop dust bring you back above zero, RHEA clears the flag on her own, or you can tap **Clear it** on the Planet deck. The pill then reads **Stable**.

<Warning>
If upkeep keeps outrunning production, your troops pay for it. Within the hour, RHEA settles the debt:
</Warning>

1. **Your tank pays first.** If that covers it, the flag clears and no troop dies.
2. **Then your cheapest troops starve:** raiders first, then defenders, then heavies. Each one pays its training price: 200, 300 or 350 dust. Only enough die to bring your balance back to exactly 0.
3. **If your whole garrison can't cover it,** every troop at home starves and your balance becomes 0. Stationed troops go home first: allies' troops on your planet fly home alive, and your troops with allies are called back. Troops in training or on the way home starve once they're back.

You get a **Troops starved** alert. Your dust then starts again from 0. The army settles at a size your planet can feed, so check **Upkeep** against **Production** before you train.
