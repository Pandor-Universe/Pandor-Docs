Your planet is your whole game. It makes your dust, trains your fleet and holds your defence. Each wallet gets one.

## Claiming your planet

After you sign in, you choose two things:

- **A quadrant:** North-east, North-west, South-east or South-west. Your planet lands there.
- **A name:** up to 32 characters.

Claiming is free. You pay nothing, and your wallet doesn't sign a transaction. The wallet you signed in with becomes your planet's payout wallet: see [What you sign](/wallet/what-you-sign).

Your species and rarity are rolled at random. You don't pick them. Every new planet starts the same way:

| | At the start |
|---|---|
| Production level (Dust Reactor) | 1 |
| Defence level (Bastion building) | 0 |
| Troops | None |
| Dust | 25,000 |

## Species

There are four species, each equally likely: a 1 in 4 chance. Your species gives your planet one bonus.

| Species | Bonus | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Ferox | +20% attack | Your troops hit harder. |
| Vex | +25% speed | Your fleets fly faster: about 2.56 seconds per map unit instead of 3.2. |
| Terra | +25% production | Your planet makes more dust. |
| Bastion | +25% defence | Your troops defend harder. The flat defence from your Bastion building stays the same. |

Your species also names your troops. A Terra world trains Falcons, Titans and Aegis. See [Fleet](/war/fleet) for every species.

<Note>
Bastion is both a species and the name of your defence building. Every planet has a Bastion building, whatever its species.
</Note>

## Rarity

| Rarity | Chance | Production |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common | 79.7% | ×1.00 |
| Rare | 16.0% | ×1.05 |
| Legendary | 4.3% | ×1.10 |

Rarity changes production only. It doesn't touch attack, defence or speed. The app names your planet by both, for example "Rare Terra world".

If your wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token, your planet is never Common. It lands Rare, with a 1 in 5 chance of Legendary. See [Seeker Genesis](/seeker-genesis).

## Levels

Your planet has two levels. Each goes up to 20.

- **Production level**, raised at the **Dust Reactor**. Your dust per minute grows with it: level 10 makes ten times what level 1 makes. It also adds 20% per level above 1 to the dust you get for the pORE your real mining packs earn. Founding a coalition needs level 5.
- **Defence level**, raised at the **Bastion building**. Each level adds a flat 500 defence to every battle on your planet, even when no troops are home.

Costs and build times are on [Upgrades](/planet/upgrades). Dust rates are on [Dust](/planet/dust).

## Where your planet lands

New planets land close together, in the quadrant each owner picked. The cluster spreads wider as more planets join. Once placed, a planet never moves.

So your neighbours are near. With 100 planets in the galaxy, two planets are typically about 228 map units apart: about 12 minutes of flight. See [travel times](/war/raids#travel-time).

## What others can see

- **In the app:** the Galaxy chart shows other players your planet's name, its distance from them and its production level, labelled "Development". Nothing else.
- **On Solana:** your levels, your tank and your total upkeep are public. Anyone can read them.
- **Encrypted:** your balance and your troop counts. A battle can reveal them: see [Defending](/war/raids#defending).

## Your planet can't be taken

Raids and assaults take dust and destroy troops. They never capture or destroy a planet. Your world stays yours.

## Your planet NFT

A planet can also be held in your wallet as an NFT in the **PANDOR Planets** collection. The app shows it under **Your planet NFT**. It carries your planet's species and rarity, and the game pays its cost, not you.

For now it's bound to you: it can't be transferred, sold or burned.
