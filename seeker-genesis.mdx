If your payout wallet holds a **Seeker Genesis Token**, your planet gets three perks. PANDOR checks the token on chain, and the perks arrive on their own, with nothing extra to sign.

| Perk | What you get | How often |
|---|---|---|
| A better planet | Rare or Legendary, never Common | Once per token, ever |
| Premium trial | 7 days of premium | Once per token, ever |
| Free Supply Drops | Your free drops topped up to 10 | Once per token, each round |

## The token must be in your payout wallet

Your payout wallet is the wallet you sign in with when you claim your planet. Every perk checks that this wallet holds the token. See [What you sign](/wallet/what-you-sign#your-payout-wallet).

The perks are counted per token, not per wallet or planet. A token that has already used a perk can't use it again, on any planet.

## A better planet

Claim your planet with the token in your payout wallet. The app's button reads **Claim my Genesis world**. Your planet then rolls Rare or Legendary, never Common.

| Rarity | With Seeker Genesis | Everyone else |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common | never | about 79.7% |
| Rare | 80% | about 16.0% |
| Legendary | 20% | about 4.3% |

Rarity raises your planet's dust production: Rare by 5%, Legendary by 10%. See [Planets](/planet/planets).

If your planet's roll doesn't complete, the perk isn't used up, and you can try again.

## Seven days of premium

Your planet gets **7 days of premium**, added on top of any premium it already has. It arrives with no wallet prompt when you claim your planet, or the first time you sign in if your planet already exists. See [Premium](/ore/premium) for what it unlocks.

## Free Supply Drops

Each round, your token tops your planet up to **10 free Supply Drops**. It never takes you past 10 and never lowers what you have: 5 left becomes 10, and 0 becomes 10.

- Free drops are used before any SKR. A roll made only of free drops never touches your SKR.
- The top-up happens on its own, with no prompt: when you claim your planet, and when the app checks your drops.
- Rounds aren't on a timer. PANDOR opens each new round, and each round lets your token top you up once.

The app shows your free drops as **Free · N**. See [Supply Drops](/ore/supply-drops).

<Tip>
Use your free drops. If you still hold 10 when a new round opens, that round's top-up adds nothing, and the round is spent anyway.
</Tip>
